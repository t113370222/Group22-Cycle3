• short final conclusion.Our analysis using a two-proportion z-test shows a statistically significant difference in current cigarette use between students who felt sad/hopeless and those who did not (p < 0.05). Students with sad/hopeless feelings have a higher proportion of cigarette use, with an estimated difference of approximately 11.5%. This suggests that mental well-being is a meaningful factor associated with smoking behaviors among youth.
